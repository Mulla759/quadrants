06 — LOCAL-FIRST
Yours. On this device.
Quadrant runs entirely in your browser. There’s no account and no server holding your lists — everything stays on your machine, and leaves only when you export it.
No account
Open it and start. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into.
Works offline
Every feature works with the network off. Install it like an app.
Plain JSON export
Take a map anywhere, any time — and import it back just as easily.
No tracking
No analytics, no ads, no network calls. Just your lists.
